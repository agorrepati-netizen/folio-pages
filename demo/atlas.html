Method

Every figure is queried live from Adobe Customer Journey Analytics, data view "CJA B2B Edition Demo", in GMT, each time this page opens. Each number is linked to the query that produced it, and you can inspect any of them in Sources.

Win rate
Deals won ÷ (deals won + deals lost) in the period. Used the same way everywhere on the page.
Average won deal
Closed-won revenue ÷ closed-won deals.
Opportunities created
Opportunity-created events in the period. Treated as the lead indicator for revenue; see the fourth-quarter range for how closely it has tracked.
Fourth-quarter range
Closed-won revenue fitted on opportunities created three months earlier, across every complete month from April 2025. The range is about an 80% band from the fit’s monthly error. A guide, not a forecast of record.
Months of cover
Distinct opportunities with any event in the month ÷ deals closed (won or lost) that month. Active opportunities are distinct counts, so months do not add up to a year.
Pace needed
2025 fourth-quarter revenue minus 2026 fourth-quarter revenue so far, spread over the days left in the quarter and shown per 30 days.
Comparisons
Complete months only. The current month is shown separately and left out of year-on-year figures.
Funnel
Counts of stage events, not a cohort of deals followed through. Losses before proposal = lost deals − (proposals − wins), assuming one proposal per opportunity. Days in stages adds the average days per event at opportunity creation, needs assessment and solution presented.
Meetings per opportunity
Meeting events ÷ distinct opportunities. Across industries it tracks win rate almost exactly (correlation ), which points to a mechanical link: won deals pass through more stages and log more meetings.
Account segments
Company size, propensity to buy and propensity to churn come from the company profile on each account. Revenue per account counts accounts with any activity in 2026.
Data check
The fall in new opportunities shows in every top-of-funnel event at once, while closed deals and active people continue, so it is not a gap in data collection. 2025 shows the same trough in its spring.
